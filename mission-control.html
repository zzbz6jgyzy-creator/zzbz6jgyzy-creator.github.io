BEHIND THE VIEW

A closer look at home.

This interactive study combines NASA imagery with a three-dimensional surface, a separate cloud layer and cinematic atmospheric lighting.

The surface and cloud maps are static satellite imagery, not live weather. The lighting views are artistic presets rather than a representation of the Sun’s current position. Rotation is accelerated for this visual experience.

Detail that adapts to your screen.

Auto balances detail and performance. High requests a 4K surface; Ultra requests 8K when your device supports it. Your graphics hardware may limit the available resolution. You can pause rotation at any time.
