    Mission manual Controls, tactics & scoring
    01

    Keep it upright

    Tap left or right to counter drift. The attitude instrument shows how close you are to the allowable tilt.

    02

    Read the window

    The countdown is the time remaining to the ignition window. When it opens, the Stage button and cue illuminate.

    03

    Light the ship

    Press Stage, or Space, while the window is open. The best scores come from the centre of the window with a level stack.

    A / D or arrow keys: steer · Space: stage · P: pause · M: sound. All controls also work by touch. Scores and progress are saved on this device.
