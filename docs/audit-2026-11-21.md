## Fixed

1. **Cybercab guide** (`data/cybercab-guide.json`, `cybercab-guide.html`). It said Tesla "has published no order page, price or delivery terms". Since 13 Nov Tesla has invited select customers to order: $29,990 before tax, $1,000 non-refundable deposit, a required $299-a-month Autonomy Subscription, estimated delivery December 2026 (Electrek, opened 21 Nov, quoting Tesla's own posted images). Public Cybercab rides also opened in Dallas on 19 Nov (Teslarati, opened 21 Nov). Updated the price and customers figures, three FAQ answers and the timeline, added both sources, set "reviewed" to 21 Nov and added a visible Correction entry. Both facts are labelled Reported because we have not opened Tesla's own posts ourselves.

## Needs a decision

1. **Starlink orbits are stuck at 17 Nov.** CelesTrak returns HTTP 403 to GitHub's runners on every refresh since 18 Nov. The workflow step has `continue-on-error`, so the run shows green while the satellite catalogue and the "identify a satellite" tracking do not change. The headline counts are unaffected (they come from McDowell) and the pages state "orbits as of 17 Nov". I did not work around CelesTrak's block. Options: wait for it to lift, or switch to another orbit source.
2. **Search titles over 70 characters.** About 40 Dispatch articles published 9 to 20 Nov carry a `seoTitle` equal to the full headline (some over 120 characters), and `tests/test_seo_audit.py::test_article_seo_titles_reach_the_title_tag` has been failing on main because of it. Google cuts titles near 60 characters. Fix is a short hand-written `seoTitle` per article; headlines stay as written.
3. **`home-stats.json` is unused.** Last updated 16 Oct (Starlink 11,154). No page loads it. Safe to delete.
