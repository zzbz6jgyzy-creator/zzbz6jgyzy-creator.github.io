## Fixed in this PR

1. **Starship schedule entry (F8)** in `starship-data.js`: Musk said "4 to 6 weeks" at the X Takeover event on **10 Oct** (7 to 21 Nov from that day) and separately guessed a launch a little before Thanksgiving (26 Nov). The entry said "roughly late November" for the first figure and was dated 11 Oct. Now states both, with the event date.
2. **Flight 15 objective (F17)** in `starship-data.js`: said "catch or controlled splashdown" for the booster. SpaceX said on the 20 Oct call it will attempt both catches. Reworded, with the licence status.
3. **Older articles still stating NET 8 Nov (F19)**: `dispatch/starship-moon-refuel-22-vs-8.html` and `dispatch/booster-22-static-fire.html` now carry a visible "Update, 5 Nov" with the 12 Nov marker. `dateModified` set. (`ship-42-first-tower-catch` was corrected yesterday.)
4. **Norway (F11)** in `data/fsd-europe.json`: context said "check the dated authority sources". Statens vegvesen (page updated 29 Oct, read again today) says not approved, not considering earlier introduction while UN R171 is unmet, customer demonstrations not yet allowed. Status stays "unknown" because the model has no "not approved" value.
5. **`tests/fsd-europe.test.mjs`**: main failed this test since the 09:40 RDW publish, which set the Netherlands `conditions.hardware` to `HW4+` (correct, from the RDW notice) while the test required `null`. The test now requires an authority source for any hardware condition, which is what the old rule protected.

## Notes and open items (not changed)

1. **"Newest build reporting" can read as "highest version".** By the site's documented rule (newest first-seen day, then version) 2026.38.5.12 is right, and Teslascope's own "latest" agrees. But 2026.39.6 (38 cars, first seen 29 Oct) is a higher number. Consider "most recently first seen build".
2. **Texas article quotes CNBC as "shed 7%".** CNBC's current page says SpaceX was "down 4% after the open"; 7% may be its earlier premarket version. The article labels these as premarket figures from different times. No change.
3. **Dispatch card for the 21 Oct Starship timeline** says "next month". It carries its own 21 Oct date, so it is dated, but "in November" would age better.
4. **Still open from yesterday**: F10 (Florida note, tx-model-y-124 date, 40 "Modey Y" VINs), F12/F13 (LT, BE, SK "recorded" vs "media"; "as of" labels), F14/F15 (Flight 14 timing and "worked" vs "partial"), F16 (USSF-481 two times), F20 (UK Model 3 figures). F14 and F15 need your choice of source of truth.
5. **Could not read directly**: tesla.com (403), apnews.com, x.ai/grok (403), fsddb model split, the Tesla non-EU FSD market list. Facts that depend only on those are marked unverified in yesterday's tables.

The two oldest are `fsd-tracker.html` "As of 19 Feb 2026" and `optimus.html` "As of 22 Apr 2026"; I did not re-verify what they refer to, so treat them as unchecked.
