# 11 Nov 2026 — Finland Traficom recognition

Primary: Traficom English notice, 11 Nov 2026 07:53, decision date **10 Nov 2026**.
https://www.traficom.fi/en/news/teslas-driver-assistance-system-now-permitted-finnish-roads

Finnish original:
https://www.traficom.fi/fi/uutiset/teslan-kuljettajaa-avustava-jarjestelma-nyt-sallittu-suomen-tieliikenteessa

What the notice establishes:

- Traficom recognised the Dutch (RDW) provisional type-approval for Tesla Assisted Driving.
- The system may be used as driver assistance on Finnish roads.
- Tesla can immediately start offering it to Finnish customers.
- Driver remains fully responsible; winter camera obstruction can limit the system; the 50% overspeed setting is gone and speed-offset settings reset at each start.
- National recognition does not bind Finland’s TCMV vote. EU-wide approval is still a separate winter process.

What it does **not** establish:

- Tesla’s own market listing (manufacturer pages remain 403 to this tracker; last independent list check did not include Finland).
- First customer installation / OTA observation.
- An EU committee vote or a hardware generation (the 11 Nov notice does not restate HW4+).
