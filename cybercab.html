Cybercab vs Robotaxi

Two different vehicles. A Cybercab is Tesla’s purpose-built two-seater with no steering wheel or pedals. A Robotaxi (Model Y) is a regular Model Y running Tesla’s ride-hail service. Cybercab counts come from the Texas DMV registry, the only state that publishes one. Robotaxi totals add Texas registrations to estimates from other states. A registration isn’t the same as a car in service.
