Earnings

Q3 results are out

Tesla reported Q3 2026 after the close on 21 October: revenue of $28.05B (flat year on year), GAAP net income of $787M (down 43%), non-GAAP EPS of $0.48, and free cash flow of $1.53B after capex of $4.73B. Cash and investments ended at $45.4B. Tesla now guides to about $20B of 2026 capex (Tesla Q3 update · write-up). Replay on ir.tesla.com.

SpaceX reported a day earlier, after the close on 20 October: Q3 2026 revenue of $14.58B and net income of $4.40B (write-up). 20 Oct · 3:30 p.m. CT · SpaceX IR · @SpaceX

Last print

Q3 deliveries already out

2 October: 486,532 vehicles delivered, 464,391 produced, 13.7 GWh of energy storage deployed. Model 3/Y did the work — 478,237 of the deliveries. Next delivery print is expected in early January.
