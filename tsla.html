Know the language

Make the terminology click.

Revenue

Sales recognised during the reporting period. It is not the same as cash received, profit or a company’s stock-market value.

Operating income & margin

Operating income is revenue after operating costs and expenses, before items such as interest and taxes. Operating margin divides operating income by revenue.

GAAP net income

Profit or loss under generally accepted accounting principles, after non-operating items and taxes. Tesla’s figures here are attributable to common stockholders; SpaceX uses its reported consolidated net income.

Operating cash flow

Cash generated or used by operating activities during the stated period. Working-capital timing means it can differ substantially from net income.

Capital expenditure

Spending on long-lived assets, using each issuer’s stated definition. It is a cash-use measure rather than a deduction from revenue in the same period.

Free cash flow

Often calculated as operating cash flow less capital expenditure. Definitions can differ. The Tesla bridge uses Tesla’s reported measure; the SpaceX bridge labels an editorial subtraction and its nine-month period.

Adjusted EBITDA

A non-GAAP measure with issuer-specific adjustments. It does not equal net income or free cash flow. Read the issuer’s reconciliation before comparing companies.

Share price & market capitalisation

A share price is the price of one share. Market capitalisation uses a relevant share count as well. Comparing two share prices alone does not compare the size or value of the businesses.

Sources, data timing & how this page updates

Two different kinds of data

Market prices belong to the external TradingView chart, which has its own availability, delay and timestamp. Company results are saved figures reviewed 25 Oct 2026. A release date is not the time of the last stock trade. No market-cap or valuation figure is inferred from a share price alone.

Original company reports

Tesla: Financial release · Investor Relations · Nasdaq instrument page . Reporting period: Q3 2026; source published 21 Oct 2026.

SpaceX: Financial release · Investor Relations · Nasdaq instrument page . Reporting period: Q3 2026; source published 20 Oct 2026.

Tesla net income is attributable to common stockholders. SpaceX net income follows the consolidated release. Capex definitions and non-GAAP measures can differ between issuers. The revenue mix reconciles to the reported total for each company. Percentage changes are calculated from the saved USD-million figures, so they can differ from percentages rounded to whole numbers in a release. Negative or zero comparison bases do not produce a misleading percentage.

Cash-flow periods stay explicit

The Tesla bridge is company-reported Q3 free cash flow. The SpaceX bridge subtracts reported nine-month capex from nine-month operating cash flow. It is an editorial calculation, not reported quarterly free cash flow. Missing quarterly fields are not filled from year-to-date values.

Updates and saved views

Financial data are updated after reviewing an original company release. The site build refreshes this page, its CSV, financial-calendar records and the original-reporting selection. The expected reporting window in the shared calendar retains its source precision. Unconfirmed earnings dates stay unconfirmed.

Your selected company and financial-chart measure are saved in this browser when available; they do not sync with your member account. Calculator inputs are never saved to browser storage. The chart contacts TradingView only when opened.
