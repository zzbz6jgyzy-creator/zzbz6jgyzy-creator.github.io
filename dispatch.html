A space jellyfish is a rocket plume lit by sunlight while the ground is still dark. The best window is 30 to 60 minutes before sunrise or after sunset

A space jellyfish forms when a rocket's high-altitude exhaust is still in sunlight and the observer is already in darkness. Vandenberg officials, who also call it twilight phenomenon, say it usually appears two to three minutes after a launch 30 to 60 minutes before sunrise or after sunset. USSF-380 this Saturday is a night launch, not a twilight one.
