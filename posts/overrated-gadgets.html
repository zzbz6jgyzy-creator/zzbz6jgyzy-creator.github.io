Overrated gadgets right now

5 min read Updated for 2026
Disclosure: Some links below may be affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission. Rankings are based on usefulness, not payout.

Every season, tech marketing invents a new object you’re supposed to feel incomplete without. Most of those objects are competent. Very few are necessary. This list is for the moment before you click “buy now” on something that will become a paperweight with Bluetooth.

1. AI pin / wearable chatbot theater

Cool demo. Rough daily life. You’re paying for novelty, cloud dependency, and a conversation you could have with the phone already in your pocket.

Buy instead: A solid pair of open-ear or reliable earbuds + the notes app you already ignore less. Buy earbuds instead →

2. Ultra-premium earbuds with tiny yearly upgrades

If you already own last year’s flagship buds, the “new” model is often a firmware story with better packaging. Marginal ANC gains rarely justify the full repurchase.

Buy instead: Midrange buds with a proven fit, or replacement tips/wings for the pair you already like. Buy midrange earbuds →

3. Smart gadgets that require a subscription to stay smart

A camera, lock, or sensor that bricks features behind a monthly fee is not a gadget. It’s a landlord.

Buy instead: Local-friendly / Matter-capable gear that still works when the marketing team changes pricing. Buy Matter smart plug →

4. Gimmick desk toys posing as productivity tools

RGB everything. Tiny secondary screens that show your CPU graph. Kinetic sculptures that collect dust faster than they collect focus.

Buy instead: A monitor arm, a decent desk mat, or a lamp with sane brightness. Boring wins. Buy a monitor arm →

The actual move

Skip the launch. Fix one friction point: charging clutter, bad posture, weak mic, dead cables. That’s the upgrade you’ll still thank yourself for in six months.
