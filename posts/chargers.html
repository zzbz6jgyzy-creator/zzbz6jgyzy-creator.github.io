Buy list

Chargers that aren’t a scam

5 min read Power
Disclosure: Some links may be affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you buy through them.

Charger marketing loves giant wattage numbers. Your laptop does not need a portable fusion reactor. It needs honest ports, sane heat, and a cable that can actually deliver the power.

Skip: mystery 100W bricks with no brand trail

If the listing is mostly stock photos and adjectives, skip it. Power adapters are not the place to gamble on “almost USB-PD.”

Buy: one compact GaN charger with 2–3 useful ports

Look for clear USB-PD labeling, enough combined output for phone + laptop, and reviews that mention heat after real use — not just unboxing excitement.

Starter pick pattern: 65W-class dual/triple-port GaN for most people. Step up only if you regularly charge a hungry laptop and a tablet at once. Buy 65W GaN charger →

Also buy: one cable you trust

A great charger with a weak cable is cosplay. Match cable rating to charger output.

Get: One short desk cable and one longer bag cable. Label them. Future you will cry less. Buy a rated USB-C cable →

Travel add-on

A slim power bank beats a second wall brick for cafés and trains. Prefer models with pass-through charging only if reviews confirm it doesn’t cook itself.

Get: A pocketable bank with USB-C in/out. Buy a USB-C power bank →
