About the project

Built for people who are done with launch-day theater.

SkipThisTech is a free, opinionated tech ranking site. We roast overhyped gadgets, spotlight underrated fixes, and keep recommendations short enough to act on.

What you’ll find here

Rankings, skip lists, and the occasional quiz. Not unboxing scripts. Not twenty nearly identical “best of” products. If something is a bad buy, we say skip. If something quietly solves a daily annoyance, we say buy.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on SkipThisTech are affiliate links. That means if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes the ranking order: we would rather tell you to buy nothing than push junk for a cut.

When you join Amazon Associates or other programs, replace the placeholder buy links in each post with your tracked URLs. Keep a clear disclosure near every recommendation.

Zero-budget by design

This site is static HTML/CSS/JS so you can host it free on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. No premium theme. No monthly hosting bill. Just publish rankings and send people here from Reddit, Pinterest, or short-form video.
