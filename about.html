About

Fan account. Owner. Shareholder.

The X bio is the whole brief: Tesla, SpaceX and Grok. News, numbers and launch footage. Not affiliated with Tesla, SpaceX or xAI.

I am Alex. On X I post as @AlJR86, under the name Fan | Alex · Tesla, SpaceX & Grok. The account opened in September 2024. It is a fan account in the literal sense: I do not work for these companies, I do not speak for them, and I do not get a briefing before a launch or a software night.

What I do have is the product and the stock. I drive a Tesla — Beast — in Ireland. I hold Tesla shares. I watch every Starship attempt the way other people watch a league table. And I actually run Grok bots against my day: one for the car, one for the news, one for the gym, one that trades against me for sport.

Why a website

X is where the footage lands first. A post can carry a number, a clip, and a sentence. It cannot carry the Irish aside, the owner note, and the second number without turning into a thread people skip. This site is the expansion of that profile: the same three desks, written so you can sit with them.

If you found this from a post, the original is linked at the bottom of each dispatch. The live wire stays on X. This is the filing cabinet.

Ireland, Dublin, Beast

A lot of Tesla coverage is written as if every owner lives next to a Supercharger in California. I do not. Irish roads are the final boss I keep mentioning: frost, potholes, village lanes, and an FSD map that has not officially arrived. When I ask Tesla for climate that reads outside temperature, or joke that Halloween Mode looks like an Optimus hand, that is the owner talking — not a press desk.

Same for the Boring Company note. Dublin has been talking about MetroLink for years. Vegas Loop is already moving people. That gap is why I asked, on the record, whether there is a job in Dublin.
