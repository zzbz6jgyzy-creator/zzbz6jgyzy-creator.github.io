Recent logged consumption can be used as a starting point. Adjust for the journey you have in mind.

An energy budget, not route guidance. No terrain, weather, charger stops or battery temperature are modelled. Use Tesla Trip Planner for travel. Comparisons cover energy only, not total ownership cost.

YOUR CAR SETS THE SCHEDULE

Check the maintenance summary first.

Use Controls → Service → Maintenance where supported, or your vehicle’s manual. Filters, tyres, brakes and servicing depend on your model, equipment, driving and climate. Set the reminder dates and distances that apply to your car.

These reminders stay in this browser and appear when you visit. They do not send notifications or book service.
