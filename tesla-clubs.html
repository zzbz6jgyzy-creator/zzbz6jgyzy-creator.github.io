03 / Make the first connection

A club for the drives.
A community for the everyday.

The right fit can be a national network, a local chapter or a conversation online. Here is how to tell them apart and take the next step.

01

45 listings

Owners clubs

National or regional organisations listed as owners clubs in the directory audit. Start with their own site for membership options and local contacts.

02

94 listings

Regional chapters

Local groups or chapters, often within a larger club. Look for the parent group in a listing, and ask which chapter covers your area.

03

35 listings

Independent communities

Owner-run forums, social groups and other independent communities. Check their joining rules, moderation and whether they meet in person.

Your first hello

Three small steps.
A much bigger ownership experience.

  1. Find a group that fits.Use its region and parent-club information as a starting point. Ask the organiser about coverage and membership before signing up.
  2. Check the next real event.Use the group’s own calendar or contact channel for dates, venue, accessibility, charging plans and any booking requirements.
  3. Introduce yourself.Mention your area, your car or your interest in Tesla, and what you would enjoy: a first meetup, a road trip or advice from fellow owners.

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need to own a Tesla?

It depends on the group. Some welcome prospective owners or other EV enthusiasts; others restrict membership. Check the group’s current joining rules rather than assuming.

Does being listed mean Tesla officially recognises a club?

No. Directory categories describe the publication audit. A listing is not proof of current Tesla recognition, endorsement or affiliation. Check the club and Tesla’s own programme for that.

Is joining free?

Exploring this directory is free. Each group decides its own membership fees, benefits and event charges. Joining Supercharged Daily does not enrol you in any club.

Does a map point tell me where a club meets?

No. The map shows approximate country reference points, and location suggestions use coarse regional references. They are not addresses, live meetups or driving directions.

Will my saved clubs sync with my account?

Saved listings and browsing preferences stay in this browser when storage is available. They do not sync with your member account. No device coordinates or city queries are saved.

What if a group is missing or a link has changed?

Use the correction link below and include the club name, country, public source and listing reference if there is one. Changes need editorial review before they are published.
