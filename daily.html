Starlink / 27 Oct 2026

easyJet said Starlink’s numbers didn’t work. Nine months later it’s putting it on its whole Airbus fleet

In January easyJet’s CEO said Starlink’s economics weren’t working. On 26 Oct it said Starlink goes on 277 A320-family jets from 2027. What changed, and the Apollo link.

Starlink / 27 Oct 2026

Verizon says Starlink Mobile needs up to $500 billion and 4 to 6 years. Musk says 2 or 3

Verizon’s CEO put a $300B–$500B, four-to-six-year price on a SpaceX phone network on 26 Oct. Musk replied “2 or 3 years.” What each side said, and the math.

SpaceX AI / 26 Oct 2026

SpaceX’s $40 billion GPU deal is four times the old record. Musk called it closed; the filing says not yet

SpaceX’s 8-K, filed 26 Oct, shows a binding commitment for a ~$40B Apollo-managed GPU lease. The rate isn’t set and closing is expected before year-end.

Tesla / 26 Oct 2026

Latvia approves Tesla’s supervised FSD, making it 10 EU countries. Here’s what drivers there are getting

Latvia’s CSDD accepted the Dutch approval on 26 Oct, with tighter speed and driver-monitoring rules. Tesla says rollout begins soon; no date or Latvian price yet.

Grok / 26 Oct 2026

Musk says Grok Bot can now phone a business for you. Here’s what’s confirmed and what isn’t

Musk says the Bot can call a person or business and send you a summary. Prerelease builds show a Calls panel; the official changelog doesn’t mention calling yet.

SpaceX / 25 Oct 2026

Booster 22 just fired all 33 engines. Here’s what still stands between Starship and its first double catch

SpaceX confirmed a full-duration 33-engine static fire for Flight 15’s booster on 25 Oct. Ship 42, the FAA licence and a launch date are still open.
