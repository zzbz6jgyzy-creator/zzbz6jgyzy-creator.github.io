Stories for your day.

Starlink / 29 Oct 2026

The FCC votes today on opening 482 MHz more spectrum to satellite-to-phone service, and AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile want their busiest 5G bands kept out

The FCC votes today on a proposal adding 482 MHz for direct-to-device services like Starlink Mobile, raising eligible bands from 5 to 13. The big carriers want 2.5 and 3.45 GHz out.

Roadster / 29 Oct 2026

Tesla's new Roadster is on display at SpaceX's Hawthorne HQ, parked under the Falcon 9 booster

Tesla brought the next-gen Roadster to SpaceX's Hawthorne headquarters on 28 Oct, parking it under the first Falcon 9 stage to land after an orbital launch.

Starship / 29 Oct 2026

Musk says Ship 42 will be the first Starship caught by the tower, now that its static fires are done

SpaceX says Ship 42 finished its static fires for Flight 15, and Musk says it will be the first Starship caught by the tower. No launch date or FAA licence yet.

Grok / 29 Oct 2026

Amazon locked Grok Bot out. Shopify just made it one of three AI agents that can pay almost anywhere with Shop Pay

Grok Bot 0.78.1 adds product cards and Shop Pay in the Vault. Shopify now names it one of three AI agents with Shop Pay for brands off Shopify, two weeks after Amazon users saw a block notice.

SpaceX AI / 29 Oct 2026

Anthropic’s IPO filing puts its SpaceX compute bill at $3.1 billion a month, 2.5 times what SpaceX disclosed in June

Anthropic’s S-1 says it agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion with SpaceXAI, about $3.1 billion a month at full capacity. It’s a ceiling, and either side can exit on 90 days’ notice from 2027.

Robotaxi / 28 Oct 2026

Cybercabs now outnumber Model Ys in Tesla’s Texas robotaxi fleet

Texas DMV records list 573 Cybercabs and 507 Model Ys under Tesla Robotaxi LLC. Tesla’s fleet is now 89% the size of Waymo’s in the state.
