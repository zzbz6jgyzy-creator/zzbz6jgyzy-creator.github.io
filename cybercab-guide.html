Tesla / Cybercab guide

Cybercab, checked against the sources

Tesla’s purpose-built robotaxi has no steering wheel, no pedals and, by Tesla’s own deck, started carrying Austin riders on 3 September 2026. This guide separates what Tesla has disclosed from what has only been reported and what is still a target.

Reviewed 27 Oct 2026 · Live fleet counters and cities →

StatusVersionsTimelinePromisesEvidenceQuestionsChangesSources

01

State of play

Six numbers that matter, each with its label, date and source.

  • Disclosed Tesla or a regulator said it in a document we opened.
  • Target A stated aim or date. Not a result.
  • Reported A journalist’s or reviewer’s finding. Tesla has not confirmed it.

Cybercab rides in Austin

3 Sep 2026start date

Tesla's Q3 deck: "We started providing Robotaxi rides using Cybercabs on September 3 in Austin." Public booking opened the next day, per Not a Tesla App.

Disclosed As of 21 Oct 2026 Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 →

Installed capacity, Giga Texas

>125,000vehicles a year

Tesla's own footnote: "Installed capacity ≠ current production rate." Tesla has not published how many Cybercabs it has built.

Disclosed As of 21 Oct 2026 Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 →

Cybercabs serving Austin

300+company claim

Tesla's Robotaxi account posted "300+ Cybercabs serving Austin – an 8x increase in 30 days". It is a count of vehicles in service, not of deliveries, and Tesla gave no breakdown.

Disclosed As of 10 Oct 2026 Tesla Robotaxi on X, 10 Oct 2026 →

NHTSA audit of the certification

AQ26002open since 3 Sep

NHTSA opened an audit query into the process Tesla relied on to certify a car with no manual controls. Not a Tesla App reports 21 requests, due 30 Oct after a 30-day extension.

Disclosed As of 8 Oct 2026 NHTSA, 3 Sept 2026 →

Cybercabs for customers

By end of 2026stated aim

On the Q3 call a Tesla speaker said: "we're trying to get Cybercabs to customers by the end of this year, 2026". Today every Cybercab built goes to Tesla's own fleet.

Target As of 21 Oct 2026 The Motley Fool, 22 Oct 2026 →

Purchase price target

<$30,000set in Oct 2024

At the 2024 reveal Musk said he "expects the cost to be below $30,000". Tesla has published no price since.

Target As of 10 Oct 2024 The Drive, 11 Oct 2024 →

Timeline

From the reveal to the open regulator question, newest last.

  1. 2024

    1. 10 Oct 2024 Milestone

      Cybercab shown at We Robot

      Tesla shows a two-seat coupe with no steering wheel or pedals. Musk says he expects the cost to be below $30,000 and production "before 2027".

      The Drive, 11 Oct 2024 →

  2. 2026

    1. 28 Jan 2026 Target

      Production start set for April

      On the Q4 2025 call Musk says: "We expect to start production in April."

      The Motley Fool, 28 Jan 2026 →

    2. 22 Apr 2026 Tesla filing

      Q1 deck: Cybercab in pilot production

      Tesla's Q1 deck lists Cybercab at "Pilot Production" and says Cybercab is "on schedule for volume production starting in 2026".

      Tesla, 22 Apr 2026 →

    3. 15 Jun 2026 Report

      EPA documents reveal basic specs

      Car and Driver reports a 219 hp front motor, 3,113 lb curb weight and a battery of roughly 50 kWh, from documents Tesla submitted to the EPA.

      Car and Driver, 15 Jun 2026 →

    4. 22 Jul 2026 Tesla filing

      Q2 deck: production started

      Tesla says it started production of Cybercab, began engineering test drives of production cars on public roads, and began employee rides on its Giga Texas campus in July.

      Tesla, 22 Jul 2026 →

    5. 3 Sept 2026 Milestone

      Cybercab rides start in Austin

      Tesla's Q3 deck gives 3 September as the day it started providing Robotaxi rides in Cybercabs in Austin.

      Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 →

    6. 3 Sept 2026 Regulator

      NHTSA opens an audit of the certification

      NHTSA opens Audit Query AQ26002. It notes Tesla certified the Cybercab as compliant with all applicable federal standards, and that the car lacks conventional manual controls.

      NHTSA, 3 Sept 2026 →

    7. 4 Sept 2026 Milestone

      Public booking opens

      Not a Tesla App reports Tesla opened Cybercab bookings to the general public in Austin at 5 p.m. local time.

      Not a Tesla App, 4 Sept 2026 →

    8. 5 Sept 2026 Report

      No AC charging, reviewer says

      Not a Tesla App quotes Out of Spec Reviews' Kyle Conner after hands-on time in Austin: "It does not support AC charging." The 2024 reveal had promised wireless charging.

      Not a Tesla App, 5 Sept 2026 →

    9. 8 Oct 2026 Regulator

      NHTSA extends Tesla's deadline

      Not a Tesla App reports Tesla asked for eight more weeks to answer 21 requests; NHTSA granted 30 days, moving the deadline from 30 September to 30 October.

      Not a Tesla App, 8 Oct 2026 →

    10. 10 Oct 2026 Milestone

      Tesla says 300+ Cybercabs serve Austin

      Tesla's Robotaxi account says the fleet is "300+", up eightfold in 30 days.

      Tesla Robotaxi on X, 10 Oct 2026 →

    11. 21 Oct 2026 Tesla filing

      Q3 deck: capacity above 125,000 a year

      Tesla lists Cybercab installed capacity above 125,000 a year at Giga Texas, with the footnote that installed capacity is not the current production rate.

      Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 →

    12. 21 Oct 2026 Target

      Q3 call: customers by year end, Dallas next

      A Tesla speaker says it is trying to get Cybercabs to customers by the end of 2026, and that "next month, we'll be launching in Dallas" for the Robotaxi service. The transcript does not say Cybercabs specifically will serve Dallas.

      The Motley Fool, 22 Oct 2026 →

    13. 26 Oct 2026 Report

      Munro Live posts a video in which Tesla's Lars Moravy and @EricETesla walk through the Cybercab's engineering, design and technology.

      Munro Live on X, 26 Oct 2026 →

      Tesla's NHTSA response due

      The extended deadline, per Not a Tesla App, for Tesla's answers to the special order. NHTSA's findings, if any, will be added here.

      Not a Tesla App, 8 Oct 2026 →

    “we expect to be in production with the Cybercab in probably—well, I tend to be a little optimistic with the timeframes—in 2026. So, yeah. Before 2027”

    Met inside the year; how many cars exist is still unpublished.

    Latest evidence: Tesla says more than 300 Cybercabs are serving Austin. Teslarati, 11 Oct 2026 →

    Said at: The Drive → Ledger last verified 15 Oct 2026

    Delivered

    Said 28 Jan 2026 by Elon Musk. Due 30 Apr 2026.

    “We expect to start production in April.”

    The ledger counts CNET's 24 April report; Tesla's own Q1 deck still said pilot production on 22 April.

    Latest evidence: CNET reported Cybercab production had begun, at a "very slow" initial rate. CNET, 24 Apr 2026 →

    Said at: The Motley Fool → Ledger last verified 15 Oct 2026

    Missed

06

Questions

Buying, price, range, charging and legality, answered from the sources.

Can I buy a Cybercab?

Not yet. On the Q3 call a Tesla speaker said Tesla is trying to get Cybercabs to customers by the end of 2026, and that today all of them go to Tesla's own Robotaxi fleet. Tesla has published no order page, price or delivery terms.

The Motley Fool, 22 Oct 2026 →

How much will a Cybercab cost?

Tesla has not published a price. At the 2024 reveal Musk said he expected the cost to be below $30,000. That is a target from two years ago, not a quote.

The Drive, 11 Oct 2024 →

Does the Cybercab have a steering wheel or pedals?

No. NHTSA's audit notes the vehicles lack permanently attached, conventional manual controls such as a brake pedal, gas pedal, steering wheel and mirrors.

NHTSA, 3 Sept 2026 →

When did Cybercab rides start?

Tesla's Q3 deck says it started providing Robotaxi rides using Cybercabs on 3 September 2026 in Austin. Not a Tesla App reports public booking opened the next day.

Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 → · Not a Tesla App, 4 Sept 2026 →

How many Cybercabs are there?

Tesla says 300+ serve Austin (10 October). It has not published a production total. The >125,000 a year figure is installed capacity, which Tesla itself says is not the current production rate. For registrations and live counts use the live tracker.

Tesla Robotaxi on X, 10 Oct 2026 → · Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 →

What is the Cybercab's range and battery size?

Tesla has not published either. Car and Driver reports a battery of roughly 50 kWh and just under 280 miles from lab figures in an EPA filing, and calls them very rough estimates. Other outlets quote different numbers from the same filing.

Car and Driver, 15 Jun 2026 →

How does the Cybercab charge?

The 2024 reveal promised wireless charging. Not a Tesla App reports, citing a reviewer with hands-on time, that the cars in Austin use DC fast charging through a hidden NACS port and have no onboard AC charger. Tesla has not published a charging spec.

The Drive, 11 Oct 2024 → · Not a Tesla App, 5 Sept 2026 →

Is the Cybercab legal on US roads?

Tesla told NHTSA it certified the cars as compliant with all applicable federal safety standards. NHTSA opened an audit query on 3 September to examine that certification, including where Tesla decided a standard did not apply. Not a Tesla App reports Tesla's answers to 21 requests are due 30 October. An audit query is a fact-finding step, not a finding of non-compliance.

NHTSA, 3 Sept 2026 → · Not a Tesla App, 8 Oct 2026 →

Where can I ride one?

Austin, per Tesla. On the Q3 call Tesla said its Robotaxi service launches in Dallas next month; the transcript does not say whether Cybercabs will serve Dallas.

Tesla, 21 Oct 2026 → · The Motley Fool, 22 Oct 2026 →

Why does this guide say "Reported" so often?

Tesla has published no Cybercab spec sheet. Anything that comes from a news report, a filing read by a journalist or a reviewer's remark is marked Reported, never Disclosed.

Car and Driver, 15 Jun 2026 →
