FLIGHT STUDY / 01

A flight you can explore.

Flight 14 launched on 28 September 2026 with Booster 21 and Ship 41. Its first orbital mission delivered 26 Starlink V3 satellites. Both stages attempted ocean landings; there was no tower catch.

What you are seeing

Mission milestones are grounded in the sources below. Event times are approximate where independent confirmation is incomplete. The original ten-hour, six-orbit preflight plan is not shown as the completed flight.

The Ship path uses 373 archived SpaceX position samples from StarDash, with no more than 40 seconds between retained samples. Movement and altitude between those samples are interpolated. There are no measured vehicle attitudes or engine states in that archive. The booster path and altitude are modelled; its ocean landing point is illustrative. Vehicle dimensions and exterior features are an original visual study, not SpaceX CAD. Exhaust, plasma, satellite spacing, pad geometry, clouds and lighting are illustrative. The replay ends at the first archived position on the water, T+03:08:44.4. This sample time is not the precise touchdown time; the terminal animation interpolates the last position updates. Reporting records the Ship’s loss shortly after splashdown.

Take control
