04 · Engineering

How it works

Cold-gas thrusters

Tesla says the thruster system “generates 4–5x the force the tires can”, so acceleration no longer depends on tyre grip Tesla. Lars Moravy said on stage the car uses 10 thrusters fed by 600 litres of compressed air, and the tanks can be recharged TechCrunch. Electric actuators make micro adjustments within a fraction of a second to keep it stable tesla.com.

COPVs everywhere

“Every usable void in the car is packed with Carbon Fiber Overwrapped Pressure Vessels (COPVs).” More gas on board means more flight Tesla. SpaceX has used thrusters to position spacecraft for decades, and Tesla says making them work in a car took all-new hardware and controls Tesla.

Suction fans

A multi-mode fan and ground-sealing system makes enough suction to hold the car upside down, even when it is stationary, giving “massive downforce at zero speed” tesla.com.

Optimus-driven wings

In Track Mode the aero deploys. Tesla calls it “the best passive downforce package of any road car (approaching F1 levels!)”, with the wings moved by the Optimus actuator system Tesla.

SR-71 and F-35 design

Tesla says the shape is inspired by the SR-71 and F-35 Tesla. It has hidden headlights, a full-width taillight, Ano White paint based on rocket coatings, gold-tinted glass, an Ultrawhite cabin with grey microsuede, a steer-by-wire yoke and a fixed manual seat. Magnesium, titanium and carbon fibre are used throughout tesla.com.

Musk on stage

“Better than any James Bond car,” and “this will be the actual car that’s on the roads” TechCrunch. Afterwards on X: “Turns out the future does have flying cars” @elonmusk.

05 · Money

Price, reservations, production

  • Price: not announced. Musk gave no price on stage TechCrunch CNBC.
  • Performance: $5,000 refundable up front plus $45,000 within 10 days, so $50,000 in total Tesla Engadget.
  • Thrust: $250,000 reservation Engadget TechCrunch.
  • Rocket: invite only Engadget.
  • Production: starts in 2028, Musk said TechCrunch CNBC.

06 · Nine years

2017 promise to 2026 reveal

  1. Nov 2017

    A surprise unveil at the end of the Semi event. Promised: 0–60 in 1.9s, over 250 mph, 620 miles of range, from $200,000, deliveries in 2020. Deposits were $50,000, or $250,000 for one of 1,000 Founders Series cars The Verge.

  2. Jun 2018

    Musk adds the SpaceX thruster package The Verge.

  3. 2020–2024

    Delayed in 2020, 2021, 2023 and 2024 TechCrunch.

  4. 1 Oct 2026

    The planned reveal moves because of weather TechCrunch.

  5. 16 Oct 2026

    The reveal at McGregor, Texas: three trims, the flip and the flight Engadget The Verge.

  6. 2028

    Production start, per Musk CNBC.

07 · The night

The event

  • When: 01:55 Irish time on Friday 16 October (8:55pm ET / 5:55pm PT on Thursday 15 October).
  • Where: outdoors at SpaceX’s test site in McGregor, near Waco, Texas Engadget.
  • Length: under 30 minutes The Verge.
  • The stunts: up a C-shaped ramp to hang upside down, then a second run off the top with a flip and a thruster landing, with an Optimus robot in the driver’s seat for show. The stunts were programmed, so no human was driving Engadget. Then a flight over a Cybercab, Cybertruck, Semi, Model Y and Model 3 TechCrunch.
  • Replay: Tesla on YouTube and Tesla’s X broadcast.
