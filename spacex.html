Starlink

The 60-meter miss

A predicted 9,000-meter gap shrank to about 60 after another satellite burned without warning. Stargaze, Starlink’s own sensor network, is why we even have the story.

Starmind

One million AI satellites

SpaceX showed what a million AI satellites around Earth would look like. Starlink is about 11,000 today. Starmind would be roughly 90 times that. Michael Nicolls said in Antalya that the satellites will not work alone — they fly in clusters.

I posted the globe graphic because that is the only way the scale lands. A million is a word until you see the ring.

Catch

No upper stage has been caught

Every Starship upper stage so far has ended in the ocean. Morgan Stanley now says the first catch could be the biggest catalyst for SpaceX stock since the IPO. Adam Jonas kept a $300 target in a note called “Cheap and Getting Cheaper.”

I am a Tesla shareholder, not a SpaceX banker. I posted it because the sentence is the story: the holy grail is not another splashdown. It is a catch.

Florida

A pipeline for Starship, not a power plant

SpaceX wants a 32.4-mile, 16-inch natural gas line across Brevard County. A subsidiary called Coastal Connect Services would own it, tapping Florida Gas Transmission. They have booked the capacity for Starship, not for turbines on the grid.

On the ground

Starlink is already in the cabin

Someone on a United flight streamed five live college games at once — three seatbacks, a laptop, a tablet, the whole exit row — on free Starlink. United says 880+ planes by year end, then the fleet. Kazakhstan switched on direct-to-phone Starlink the same week, free for Beeline customers for now. The UK has had it since February. O2 wants £3 a month.
