Why FSD differs between cars

An FSD label does not describe every Tesla’s capabilities. Hardware, installed software, vehicle configuration and regional availability all matter. Full Self-Driving (Supervised) still requires an attentive driver.

By Alex RReference review 23 Oct 2026

Explore diagramKey ideasSources

Start with the three layers in the illustration. The comparison further down uses the same maintained version data as the global FSD tracker, with its snapshot date shown explicitly.

Driver assistance requires an attentive driver. Consult the instructions for your vehicle and region.

Explore the sequence

Three layers, one vehicle

Five stages
  1. 1Vehicle hardware
  2. 2Installed software
  3. 3Regional availability

  1. 01 · Start with the vehicle

    A model name alone does not identify its complete configuration. Build date, equipment and the information shown in the vehicle are better starting points than another owner’s video.

  2. 02 · Identify the hardware

    Hardware generations describe the vehicle’s computing and camera configuration. A newer software announcement should not be read as a promise that every hardware generation receives the same features.

  3. 03 · Check the installed software

    The vehicle software build and the FSD version are different labels. Record both when comparing release notes. A reported build in a sample is not confirmation that your car has received it.

  4. 04 · Check the region and entitlement

    Tesla says availability can depend on configuration, hardware, software, region, model and model year. A release in one market does not establish availability in another. [source]

  5. 05 · Keep the driver in the loop

    Tesla describes these features as supervised driver assistance. They do not replace the driver or make the consumer vehicle autonomous. Review the instructions for your particular car. [source]

Read a version comparison correctly

The table separates hardware, FSD version and vehicle build. Different values are highlighted so you can see what actually changed. It reports the site tracker’s snapshot; it does not certify eligibility for a particular VIN.

Use your car as the final check

The useful question is what is installed, enabled and supported on your vehicle. Use its software screen, release notes and owner’s manual alongside the tracker. The diagram deliberately makes no claim about Tesla’s internal neural-network design.

A clearer picture

Common misunderstandings

A shared name does not mean a shared release

Two cars can show the FSD brand while running different software branches or having different supported features.

Supervised is a meaningful requirement

The driver must remain attentive. A polished route demonstration does not establish that a consumer car can operate without supervision.

Plain English

Key terms

HW3 / HW4 (AI4)
Common hardware-generation labels used by the site’s tracker.
Vehicle build
The installed vehicle-software identifier, distinct from an FSD version label.
Rollout
The process of making a software release available to eligible vehicles over time.
