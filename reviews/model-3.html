Model 3

Independent review@AlJR86

Verdict

Buy if you want the cheapest path into the Tesla software stack without giving up a proper car.

9Drive
7Practicality
9Efficiency
9Europe fit
9Value

Buy if

Commuters, first-time EV buyers, and anyone who parks in European cities.

Skip if

Skip if you need a tall boot opening, seven seats, or max motorway range every week.

The pitch

Model 3 is still the cleanest expression of the Tesla idea: one screen, one pedal feel, and a body that fits Irish multi-storeys. Highland tightened the cabin and the ride. It did not invent a new brief.

On the road

Steering is light in town and settles on the motorway. Instant torque makes every gap feel available — which is also why Autopilot and FSD Supervised matter. The car is eager; the software has to keep that eagerness polite.

Europe & Ireland

This is the easy Tesla to live with here: Supercharging is dense enough on the main corridors, home charging does the real work, and the footprint behaves on village streets. Winter range is the honest conversation, not the brochure number.

Software & FSD

Same stack as the rest of the lineup. In countries where FSD Supervised is recognised, this is the cheapest seat to see what the Dutch RDW approval actually feels like. In Ireland, you are still waiting — track that on the FSD Europe page.
