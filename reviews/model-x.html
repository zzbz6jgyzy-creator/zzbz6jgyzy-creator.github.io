Verdict

Buy if you need the seats and the statement. Skip if Y already covers your life.

Buy if

Large families who want Tesla software and a third row that adults can survive.

Skip if

Skip if you park under low barriers often, or if Model Y’s cargo math already works.

The pitch

Model X is the S platform standing up. Falcon-wing doors are the party trick and the ownership conversation. Everything else is about space and pace.

On the road

Heavy, quick, composed. The dual-motor and Plaid variants erase most traffic. You feel the mass in tight car parks more than on the motorway.

Europe & Ireland

Door clearance is the local tax. Underground car parks, tight terraces, and wet Atlantic wind all make you plan the wings. When they work, school drop-off looks like a demo.

Software & FSD

Same stack, higher seating eye-line. Useful for supervised driving when it arrives. Until then, treat it as a luxury seven-seater with a Tesla brain.
