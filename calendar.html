How this is built

Public dates, no personal book

Tesla IR for earnings and deliveries. Tesla's own posts for dated events like the Roadster unveil. Launch Library 2 for Falcon, with a fallback slate if that feed is quiet. If its time differs from a confirmed launch time, we go with the confirmed time. Starship and Grok rows stay marked as planning or TBA until the company puts a clock on them.
