SpaceX / Launch

Starship Flight 15

Time unconfirmed

NET 8 November on FAA air-traffic planning slides, not a SpaceX T-0. On the 20 Oct earnings call Musk said SpaceX expects Flight 15 next month and will try to catch both the booster and the ship. Booster 22 completed a full-duration 33-engine static fire on 25 Oct, per SpaceX. NASASpaceflight and Next Spaceflight report a Ship 42 single-engine static fire at Massey’s on 26 Oct, then a six-engine, roughly 60-second full-duration fire on 27 Oct 2026, about 4:46pm CDT (about 21:46 Irish time). SpaceX hasn’t posted about the Ship 42 fires. Both Flight 15 vehicles have now static-fired. Still no FAA licence and no SpaceX date.
