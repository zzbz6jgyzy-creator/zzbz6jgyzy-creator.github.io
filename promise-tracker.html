        How we judge

        • Delivered: done by the stated deadline.
        • Delivered late: done, but after the deadline.
        • In progress: the deadline hasn't passed yet.
        • Missed: the deadline passed without it happening, even if a new date was given later.
        • Revised: the goal or plan was changed, or no deadline was given, so it can't be scored.

        "On time" is Delivered ÷ (Delivered + Delivered late + Missed). Average delay compares each late item's deadline with the date it happened. Vague deadlines ("this year", "a few months") are read as the end of that period, and each card notes the judgment calls. Spotted an error or a better source? Tell us on X. Data: promises.json.
