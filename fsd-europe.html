Where FSD is approved in Europe

Since 9 October, FSD (Supervised) is called Tesla Assisted Driving in Europe. Each red country has recognised the Dutch RDW approval. To make it legal across the EU, a TCMV vote needs 15 of 27 states and 65% of the EU population, both at once.

    Methodology & sources

    • Approved means a national authority has recognised the RDW provisional approval (Art. 39, Reg. (EU) 2018/858). Live means public reports say the customer rollout has started.
    • The vote: under Art. 39(3) the Commission needs a TCMV qualified majority, 15 of 27 states representing 65% of the EU population. National yeses are not that vote.
    • Population figures are rounded millions; the EU total used is M.
    • Sources: official or press sources first; trackers marked † (notanfsdtracker.com, tad-tracker.eu) are secondary and were used to fill gaps and cross-check. Blank fields mean we could not confirm them.
    • Dates are shown in your own time zone ().

    Tesla Assisted Driving / FSD (Supervised) is SAE Level 2. The driver must supervise at all times. Not affiliated with Tesla or the European Commission.
