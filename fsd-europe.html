Track 1 · National

Countries recognise the Dutch approval

The RDW issued a provisional Art. 39 type approval. Other member states can recognise that file on their own. That is how FSD Supervised is already on the road in 0 countries — without a Brussels vote.

Germany, France, Italy and Spain are still in review. Berlin alone is about 19% of the EU population, which is why it matters more than another small yes.

Track 2 · Brussels

TCMV votes for EU-wide recognition

The Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles is the EU comitology committee that would need a qualified majority — 15 of 27 states covering 65% of EU population — before the Commission can recognise the Dutch approval across the whole bloc.
