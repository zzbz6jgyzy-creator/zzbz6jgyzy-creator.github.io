About Irish EV Tariff Watch

How the prices are gathered, when they were last checked, and every source we use.

How the data is gathered

Every price is entered by hand from the supplier's own tariff page or price PDF, and re-checked weekly. All unit rates are in cent per kWh including 9% VAT, and standing charges are euro per year including VAT for urban customers, unless a note says otherwise. Headline rates are each supplier's advertised new-customer price, including any standard sign-up discount noted on the plan.

Each figure is labelled by how sure we are of it:

  • Supplier: read directly from the supplier's own page or PDF.
  • derived calculated by us from a supplier figure, for example an ex-VAT price × 1.09, or a published discount applied.
  • unverified we couldn't confirm it, so it's shown with a warning.

We never type in a number we haven't seen on a source. When a supplier announces a change, we add the new price with its start date, and the site switches over to it automatically on that day. Wholesale figures for the dynamic-tariff estimates are real SEMOpx day-ahead results (ROI zone) from the SEMO reports service.

What isn't covered

Not every supplier or plan is listed. Community Power (no dedicated EV plan found) isn't included. We couldn't read Flogas's discounted EV plans or PrePay Power's prices. Rural standing charges are higher than the urban figures shown. Always compare on bonkers.ie (CRU-accredited) or switcher.ie too.

Disclaimer

Irish EV Tariff Watch is free and independent. It isn't financial advice. Electricity prices are variable and change often, so always confirm the price, contract length and exit fees with the supplier before you switch. Figures marked "derived" are our calculations and "unverified" ones couldn't be confirmed.

Referral disclaimer: right now there are no referral or sponsor arrangements. The "Switch to" buttons go straight to suppliers' own websites and we earn nothing from them. In future we may earn a fee if you switch through some links, or show clearly labelled sponsor messages. That will never change the order of the rankings, which are sorted only by price.
