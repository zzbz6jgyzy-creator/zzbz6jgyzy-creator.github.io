The energy system

Generate. Store. Use.
Timing changes everything.

Solar produces when sunlight is available. Batteries store energy for later. Homes, cars and the grid consume it at different times. Tesla builds products across these connected layers.

Conceptual energy pathways, not an installation diagram. Grid connections, backup operation and export depend on local rules and system design.
