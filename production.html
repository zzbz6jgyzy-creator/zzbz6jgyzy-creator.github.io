Sources, definitions & how this desk stays up to date

Official figures, explicit dates

The quarter records come from Tesla Investor Relations production and delivery releases. Source figures checked 25 Oct 2026; factory information reviewed 25 Oct 2026. The complete saved dataset is Q1 2024 through Q3 2026. This is a dated reference, not a live production feed.

What is measured

Production counts vehicles produced in the reporting quarter; deliveries count reported vehicles delivered. Model 3/Y and Other Models retain Tesla’s groupings. Energy storage deployed is measured in GWh and is separate from factory manufacturing capacity. Q1 2024’s 4,053 MWh is converted exactly to 4.053 GWh.

Percentage change is (comparison − baseline) ÷ baseline × 100. Year-to-date comparisons use the same available quarters in each year. Positive and negative changes remain visible. Delivery minus production is a flow difference, not total inventory. A quarter’s figures do not establish individual model or factory output.

Factories and reporting boundaries

Factory capacities and statuses follow the Q3 2026 shareholder deck, page 6. Regions group several facilities; map dots are illustrative. Annual capacities retain Tesla’s “greater than” qualifiers. Early ramp and construction remain distinct from production. Tesla’s Roadster location is undetermined. Original manufacturing table

Updates and your choices

New quarters are added after the editor verifies the official release. Updating the source files rebuilds this page, the charts and the CSV through the existing site build. Factory entries are reviewed against the latest shareholder deck. There is no paid API or automatic estimate feed. Missing or unconfirmed release dates stay unconfirmed.

Your selected quarter, chart view and factory region are saved in this browser when storage is available. Comparison links encode only the two selected quarters. These choices do not sync with your member account.
