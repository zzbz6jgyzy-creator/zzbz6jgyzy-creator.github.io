Corrections

When I get something wrong

How to tell me, and what I do about it.

Tell me

If you spot a wrong number, date, name, quote or source, email superchargeddaily@gmail.com or message me on X at @ALJR86. Please include the page link, what’s wrong and, if you have one, a source.

[ALEX: how quickly you aim to reply, e.g. “within two days”. Only promise what you can keep.]

What I do

  • I check the claim against the primary source.
  • If I got a fact wrong, I fix it on the page and add a dated note saying what changed: “Correction, 25 Oct 2026: …”.
  • If the facts moved on after I published, for example a newer registry count, I add a dated “Update” note and keep the original figure with its date.
  • Spelling, grammar and broken links are fixed without a note.
  • I don’t quietly delete stories. If one has to come down, I’ll say why.

Trackers

Tracker figures change as new data comes in. Each one shows when it was last updated, in Irish time. A changed tracker figure isn’t a correction. If a tracker showed something wrong because of my mistake or a bad source, it gets a dated note.
