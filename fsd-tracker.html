02

How to read the numbers

Every figure on this page carries one of these labels, a date and a source.

  • Disclosed Tesla or a regulator said it in a document we opened.
  • Reported A journalist, analyst or tracker found it. Tesla has not confirmed it.
  • Our estimate Our own arithmetic on disclosed numbers. Shown dashed or marked.
  • Approx. from chart Read off a chart Tesla published without exact labels.
  • Target A stated aim or date. Not a result.
