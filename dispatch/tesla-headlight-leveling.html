Tesla · 11 Oct 2026

Tesla software update 2026.38 adds Dynamic Headlight Leveling, so the low beams now re-aim themselves while you drive.

From @ALJR86 Software desk

Tesla's release notes say the low beams "automatically adjust their aim based on driving conditions and nearby traffic" to give you more light and cut glare for other drivers.

  • Models: select Model 3 and Cybertruck
  • Software: 2026.38, also listed in 2026.38.3.1 and 2026.38.5
  • Settings: none, it works on its own
  • Rollout: limited so far, mostly US cars

It's a different system from the adaptive matrix high beams Tesla already has.

I'm keen to know if it reaches more Model 3s.
