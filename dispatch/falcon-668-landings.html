SpaceX has now landed a Falcon booster 668 times, and the next attempt is tomorrow morning

From Alex RSpaceX

The 668th came early Saturday, when booster B1103 touched down at Landing Zone 4 in California on its sixth flight, after putting 21 Space Development Agency satellites in orbit.

The first one stuck the landing in December 2015. Rockets used to be thrown away after every launch.

Next up is CRS-35, a Dragon cargo run to the space station carrying more than 6,300 pounds of supplies, per NASA. Liftoff is set for 6:33am ET (3:33am PT) Tuesday from Cape Canaveral.

Landing a rocket still looks unreal to me every time.
