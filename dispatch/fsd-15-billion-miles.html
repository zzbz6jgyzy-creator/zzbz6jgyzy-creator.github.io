Tesla FSD fleet passes 15.4 billion miles

From @ALJR86 FSD desk

Tesla’s FSD (Supervised) fleet has now driven 15.4 billion miles, and it adds about 35 million more every day.

That daily figure is roughly 1,400 laps of the Earth every 24 hours, based on the live counter on Tesla’s FSD safety report.

The fleet passed 15 billion on 29 September, 29 days after it hit 14 billion. About 6 billion of the total are city miles.

These are supervised miles with a driver ready to take over, not driverless ones, and the count excludes China.

The daily number is the one I can’t get my head around.

Source: tesla.com/fsd/safety
