Tesla’s production Cybercab is in Europe for the first time, pulled from its 300+ strong US robotaxi fleet for the Paris Motor Show

From @ALJR86 Robotaxi desk

Press day is today at Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, with public days Tuesday 13 to Sunday 18 October. Tesla is in Hall 7.1, stand C25.

Show director Serge Gachot told Bloomberg visitors won’t be able to ride in it. No steering wheel, no pedals, static display only. That US fleet grew 8x in its first 30 days, per @robotaxi.

Alongside it, six Model 3s are giving free FSD (Supervised) drives, a system France hasn’t approved yet. Optimus Gen 2 is on the stand too.

@DavidMoss is already at the booth with video. Tesla hasn’t announced any European Cybercab service.
