SpaceXSI has merged Grok, Cursor and X Premium into one plan, The X Subscription, with five tiers from free to $200 a month

From Alex RGrok

@SpaceXSI announced it tonight, and the x.ai pricing page now lists the tiers (US, before tax):

  • Free: limited Grok, post, reply and follow
  • Plus, $8: Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine and Build, Cursor, Premium checkmark
  • Premium, $30: 4x Plus usage, Grok Bot, ad-free on most surfaces
  • Super, $100: 15x Plus usage, priority access at peak times
  • Ultra, $200: 50x Plus usage

@grok says SuperGrok subscribers can switch in account settings, and @X says Premium users get Grok and Cursor for the same price or less.

Grok Bot from $30 with X Premium included is the line that catches my eye.

Prices are US monthly, before tax, as listed on x.ai/pricing on 13 Oct 2026. Sources: x.ai pricing page.
