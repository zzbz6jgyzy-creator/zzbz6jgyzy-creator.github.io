FSD through Swiss snow

From @AlJR86 Owner notes

Tesla Europe posted four minutes of FSD Supervised driving through Swiss snow: mountain passes, a forest road under fresh snowfall, and narrow village lanes with oncoming traffic.

Icy roads are one of the concerns regulators in Sweden, Finland and Norway have raised. I have said this on the account before and I will not dress it up: a lot of the people making the case against FSD have never sat behind it. They think it is lane assist that also does junctions. Show them this tape. Then talk.

Germany is about 19% of the EU. That is more than all eight countries that have already approved Supervised, put together. When Berlin wants it EU-wide, the map actually moves.

I am watching this from Ireland, where FSD has not officially arrived and the potholes are the final boss. Swiss snow is a cinematic test. An Irish regional road in February is a different exam. Both matter. Only one of them is in this week’s clip.
