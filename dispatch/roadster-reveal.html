Tesla’s new Roadster is finally being revealed this Thursday, October 15, nine years after the 2017 unveil promised 0-60 in 1.9 seconds

DISPATCH / About 1 min read

From Alex R Tesla

Updated 27 Oct 2026

Update, 16 Oct 2026: Tesla has now revealed the Roadster. Read what happened, with the full replay →

Tesla confirmed the date on X after moving the outdoor event from October 1 because of severe weather. Tesla then confirmed on X that the event streams live Thursday at 7:55pm Central (CDT), which is 01:55 Friday 16 Oct in Ireland and the UK.

What the 2017 unveil claimed:

  • 0-60 mph in 1.9 s
  • 620 miles of range
  • 200 kWh battery
  • Top speed over 250 mph

In June 2018 Elon Musk wrote that a “SpaceX option package” would add “~10 small rocket thrusters arranged seamlessly around car.” Cold-gas thrusters push high-pressure gas out of nozzles, which is how Falcon 9 steers its booster.

The original Roadster has been orbiting the Sun since Falcon Heavy’s first flight in 2018.

I want to see what the thrusters actually do.

Start time: 7:55pm CDT, Thursday 15 Oct (Source: Tesla on X).
