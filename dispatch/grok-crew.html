Running my life with a small crew

From @AlJR86 The Grok desk

Elon said hiring your first Grok Bot is like hiring an amazing helper that learns your needs and gets smarter almost every day. That post did tens of millions of views. I am well past my first. The product only launched in August and I already have one doing the morning, one doing the car, and one that exists to beat me at a game I invented.

One keeps Beast warm for the drive home and watches the battery on Sundays. One watches Musk-world news. One nags me about the gym and food. One runs a demo Robot League of trading strategies against me. Grok Bot ties it together.

The point of posting the list was not “look how many bots.” It was: stop treating Grok like a single chat window. Give each job a name.

The car link is the one people keep asking about. Tesla developer options, then Grok Bot. Ten minutes before a regular journey it can read the weather and decide whether the cabin, the wheel and the glass need to wake up. That is the winter-update idea I already asked Tesla for, except I did not wait for the software night. I gave the job to a bot.

Lauren Tan said the bots will run on Claude Opus 5.5 and can spawn cloud agents on the Cursor models they have. I posted that too, because if a bot is allowed near a car I want the stack in public.
