Grok Bot in the Tesla

From @AlJR86 The Grok desk

Elon said hiring your first Grok Bot is like hiring a helper that learns you. The useful version is not one chat. It is named jobs: Tesla Bot on the car, a news bot on Tesla / SpaceX / Grok, and Grok Bot routing the work.

Tesla developer options link Grok Bot to the vehicle. Ten minutes before a drive it can read the weather and wake the cabin, the wheel and the glass — or cool the car if it has been sitting in the sun.

If a bot is allowed near a Tesla, the stack should be public. Lauren Tan said the bots run on Claude Opus 5.5 and can spawn cloud agents on the Cursor models they have.

That is the Grok desk: the car, the news, the model names. The live version stays on X. The all-round setup is the Grok Bot tutorial.
