        Mission manual Controls, tactics & scoring
        01

        Read the city

        The map shows demand as pulsing dots and your coverage as a ring around each zone: green is covered, red is short. Select cabs, then tap a zone, or use a card’s + button to send the next idle cab. Charge matters: a cab under 18% cannot work.

        02

        Run the day in windows

        In campaign and daily, each day runs in four windows: morning rush, midday, evening rush and night out. Between windows you get a few seconds and a few dispatch tokens: surge a short zone, rebalance the fleet toward demand, or pay for a roadside fix after a breakdown. Pick the safe or the bold contract each morning.

        03

        Stay in business

        Buy upgrades and cabs, charge only the cabs you need at night, and keep reputation up by covering busy zones. Cash below zero or reputation under 15 ends your week early. Finish the week and the next campaign week gets harder.

        Select fleet vehicles, then a zone · 1–4: assign to zones · M: sound · No planning timer. All controls also work by touch. Scores and progress are saved on this device.
