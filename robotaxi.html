        Read the city

        Each zone shows demand, fare and assigned cabs. Select cabs, then choose a zone, or use its + button to send the next idle cab.

        Make the trade-off

        Fares change demand. Contracts pay a bonus for service in one zone. Upgrades improve capacity, charging costs or reliability.

        After each day, choose which cabs to charge. Overnight power is cheaper than a fast charge. Your planning and night phases save automatically.
