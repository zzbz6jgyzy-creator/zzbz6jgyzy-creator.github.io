SpaceX · Starship

Why does Starship use hot staging?

Ship lights while it is still on Super Heavy. The stack never coasts. That is the whole point — and why the ring looks like it is on fire. Toggle cold staging to see the wait hot staging refuses to take.

Flight 218 Nov 2023 · first hot-stage. First water deluge. Both vehicles were then lost.
Flight 14MECO at T+2:20. Hot-stage at T+2:22. Official SpaceX timeline.
Block 3First Block 3 hot-stage on Flight 12, ~T+2:30. Same family through Flight 14.

Ascent

F14 · before MECO

Thirty-three Super Heavy Raptors are doing the work. Ship is still a passenger.

No coast

Cold staging cuts the booster, waits, then lights the upper stage. Hot staging never drops the thrust. For a stack this heavy, that wait is expensive in gravity loss and attitude propellant.

More payload

Continuous acceleration is SpaceX’s published reason. We do not invent a kilogram number. The ring exists because the payload argument won.

The hardware cost

The ring has to survive Ship lighting in the booster’s plume. Flight 2 proved the staging (~T+2:49) and then lost both vehicles. Later flights kept the idea and made the ring live.

SpaceX · Super Heavy

How does a booster catch work?

The booster flies back to the tower it left. Chopsticks close on the pins. Flight 5 proved it. Everything since is the same geometry, faster. A miss still prefers splash to a tower strike.

Flight 513 Oct 2024 · B12 · first Super Heavy catch, ~T+6:56. No engine failures on the way up.
DivertIf the approach is off, SpaceX sends it to a controlled splashdown. Missing is allowed.
Flight 15First serious attempt to catch a Ship. Same chopsticks, harder timing. NET 8 Nov.

Liftoff

T+0

The chopsticks that will catch it later have just released the stack.

No legs

Landing legs are mass you fly, then a pad you pour. A catch spends that mass once, in Mechazilla, and every booster reuses it.

Catch or splash

Flight 11’s booster did a controlled splash, not a catch. Divert is a success mode when the approach is off. A bad tower hit is not.

Ship is next

Flight 15 is the first serious attempt to catch a Ship (S42 on the Pad 2 tower). SpaceX has not confirmed it as the plan of record.

Tesla · FSD

What changed between FSD releases?

Same brand, three stacks. US HW4 has Collision Evasion. HW3 is on Lite. Europe is still Assisted Driving Basic on 14.2. Pick two stacks. Then open Collision Evasion for the HW4-only note.

SAE Level 2. The driver must supervise. Europe lists the same idea as Tesla Assisted Driving. Not Tesla’s official comparison.

Tesla · HW4

How does Collision Evasion work?

It is not a separate Autopilot. On HW4 in North America, the car can switch Supervised FSD back on to try to avoid an imminent frontal crash, or if FSD looks like it was dropped by mistake. Toggle HW3 or Europe to see the step that does not exist there.

Still SAE Level 2. The driver still has to supervise. This is not unsupervised Robotaxi.

What it isHW4 can re-engage Supervised FSD for an imminent frontal crash, or an unintentional FSD drop.
WhereNorth America, 14.3.11 and 14.3.12. Not on HW3 Lite. Not on Europe 14.2.
What it is notThe notes say frontal. We do not invent side or rear coverage, or a hands-off guardian.
