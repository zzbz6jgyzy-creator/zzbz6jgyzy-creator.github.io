In the car

Ten minutes before I leave

Link Grok Bot through Tesla developer options and it can pull the car and the weather at the same time. Before a regular journey — work, home, the shop — it can precondition from the actual outside temperature instead of a clock that does not know there was a frost.

I posted the how-to because people kept asking. Ask the bot. It will walk you through it.
